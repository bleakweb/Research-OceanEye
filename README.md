# Everything, Everywhere, All at Once: AI Research & File Dump

Welcome to the repository where structure is merely a suggestion. This is the central hub for our raw files, AI research notes, and random technical deep dives, existing in no particular order. 

If you are looking for polished, production-ready release cycles, you have taken a wrong turn. If you are looking for unfiltered data, experimental models, and hardware notes, you are in the right place.

### Notable Files in the Sandbox

While most of this repo is an unstructured dump of ongoing work, a few key documents stand out and should be referenced directly:

*   **signal-chain.md**: This file covers the complete path from a voltage on an ADC pin to a sample leaving the DAC. It details every stage, parameter, and equation alongside the relevant code, specifically focusing on the RP2040 platform combined with an external parallel DAC managed via PIO and DMA. 
*   **AI Research Notes**: (Various `.md` and `.ipynb` files) - Unsorted neural network experiments, model training logs, and architecture theories.
*   **Raw Data Dumps**: Unprocessed datasets used for training and testing our models.

### Navigating the Repository

1.  **Search is your friend:** Because directories are not strictly enforced, rely heavily on your search bar to find specific scripts, terms, or concepts.
2.  **Check the commit history:** The most active research and latest ideas will always be floating at the top of the commit log.
3.  **Read the Markdown:** We try to leave breadcrumbs in the text files before dumping raw code. Check for companion files or glossary links within the documents.
